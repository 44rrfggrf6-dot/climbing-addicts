Privacy

Last updated: October 2026.

Climbing Addicts is an independent project (contact: benoit@climbingaddicts.app). Your data is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Landing site (this page)

No account, no tracking cookie. Analytics use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and never identifies you individually. The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

Beta app (climbingaddicts.app)

To run your logbook, the app stores:

  • your email address (account and sign-in);
  • your session logs: boulders and routes, grades, gyms, crews;
  • photos you attach to your logs (AI photo recognition in development).

This data is hosted with our technical providers (Supabase for app data, Cloudflare for hosting) and only used to run the logbook, stats and crews.

Delete your account

Write to benoit@climbingaddicts.app from your account email: your account and its data are deleted, within 30 days at most.
