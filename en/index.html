Holds: we reward perseverance, not grades

Every climb and every session earns holds. Stack them up and you gain altitude. Nobody climbs against anyone here: we climb together, and what matters is coming back.

A team, not a competition

No competition between climbers. The weekly boards reward regularity, perseverance and curiosity, never raw grades. Everyone can finish first at something, and nobody is shown last.

Perseverance above all

A project sent after several tries is worth almost twice a flash at your level. A project you didn't send is still worth logging: trying counts.

Coming back is the real performance

Two short sessions are worth more than one long marathon. Returning after a break is the hardest move: it's the biggest session bonus.

You only compare to yourself

The grade alone pays little: what counts is the gap to your own usual level. Above it pays more; below it pays a bit less.

Nothing is ever taken away

No negative points, no reset. One rest week a month doesn't break your streak, and a streak that stops becomes a "best run".

Every milestone is celebrated

Week streaks, a new altitude, a crew goal reached: each step is celebrated, and earns holds.

The crew: we move forward together
